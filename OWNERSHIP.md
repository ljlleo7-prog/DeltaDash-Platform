# Declaration of Originality and Ownership

本声明适用于 **Delta Dash（暂定名）** 项目之全部规则系统、机制设计、数值平衡框架、玩法流程、测试版本文件及其衍生的非商业即打即玩（PnP）内容。
This declaration applies to all rule systems, gameplay mechanics, balancing frameworks, turn structures, test files, and derivative non-commercial print-and-play (PnP) materials of the **Delta Dash (working title)** project.

---

## 一、原创性声明

## I. Declaration of Originality

本人作为本项目主设计者，确认 **Delta Dash 的核心玩法机制、系统架构、回合流程、资源管理逻辑、平衡框架及整体设计方案均由本人原创构思并主导完成**，并在开发团队协作下持续迭代优化。

As the lead designer of this project, I hereby confirm that the **core gameplay systems, structural framework, turn flow, resource management logic, balancing architecture, and overall design direction of Delta Dash were originally conceived and primarily led by me**, with continuous iteration and refinement carried out in collaboration with the development team.

除合理借鉴桌面游戏行业常见设计范式及公开可讨论的通用机制理念外，本项目当前公开版本所呈现之具体规则表达、参数结构、交互流程、模块组合方式及版本演进路线，均属于本团队独立开发成果。

Except for reasonable inspiration drawn from common tabletop game design paradigms and publicly discussable general mechanics concepts, the specific rule expressions, parameter structures, interaction flows, module compositions, and version evolution roadmap presented in the currently published version are the result of **independent development by our team**.
